About Cape Verde

Location & Geography

Cape Verde is located about 600 km off the coast of West Africa. It’s an archipelago of volcanic origin, divided into two groups: Barlavento (windward) and Sotavento (leeward). The capital city, Praia, lies on Santiago Island, which is the largest and most populated island.

History & Culture

Discovered by the Portuguese in the 15th century, Cape Verde became a cultural bridge between Africa, Europe, and the Americas. Today, the islands are known for their strong sense of identity, peaceful democracy, and love for music. The national music genre morna expresses the sodade, a feeling of longing and nostalgia that defines the Cape Verdean soul.

Language & People

The official language is Portuguese, but most people speak Kriolu, a local Creole language full of rhythm and personality. The population is warm, joyful, and proud of their culture, always ready to share their stories, food, and laughter.
