Cape Verdean Cuisine

Cachupa

Cachupa is the heart of Cape Verdean cuisine a slow-cooked stew made with corn, beans, cassava, and meat or fish. Every island and every family has their own version, making it a true symbol of Cape Verde.

Cuscuz

Cape Verdean Cuscuz is a traditional steamed cake made from cornmeal, sugar, and coconut milk. Usually served warm with butter, honey or cheese, it is a comforting breakfast loved across the islands.

Pastel de Milho

Pastel de Milho is a crispy corn-flour pastry filled with spicy tuna or other local fish. It’s one of the most popular Cape Verdean street foods, enjoyed at celebrations, family gatherings, and beaches.

Grogue

Grogue is the strong, aromatic Cape Verdean rum made from sugarcane. It is traditionally shared among friends with music and storytelling much more than a drink, it's a cultural icon.
